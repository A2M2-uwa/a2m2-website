For collaborators

What to include in a project enquiry

The most productive conversations begin with a concrete decision, measurement or prediction problem. You do not need to have the technical method worked out; that is the part we can develop together.

  • The clinical or research question, and who would use the answer.
  • The data you have, including its format, scale and any reference standard.
  • Current ethics, consent, governance or data-access arrangements.
  • A realistic definition of a useful outcome and timeframe.

Data and privacy

Do not send patient data by email

Initial enquiries should describe data at a high level only. We can discuss secure institutional processes for any project that proceeds.
