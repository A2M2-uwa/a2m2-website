Expression of interest

A focused first message is the most useful one

Before you contact us, look through the research themes and identify a problem or method that genuinely interests you. We read enquiries carefully, and specificity helps us decide whether there is a good fit.

  1. Introduce your background

    Tell us your degree, current institution and the technical or clinical skills you would bring.

  2. Show your work

    Attach a CV and academic transcript. Include a GitHub link, paper, report or project portfolio if you have one.

  3. Name the research fit

    Briefly connect your interests to one or two A²M² themes, rather than sending a generic request.

  4. Contact a co-director

    Send the material to the relevant co-director through the contact page. Formal admission and scholarship applications follow UWA’s official process.

What we value

A good fit goes beyond a programming language

Technical care

Thoughtful experimental design, reproducible implementation and an appetite for understanding methods rather than only running them.

Clinical respect

Comfort working with incomplete, variable real-world data and respect for the expertise of clinical partners and patient communities.

Clear communication

The ability to explain a method, result and limitation precisely to people outside your own discipline.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need a health or medical background?

No. Applicants from computer science, engineering, statistics and related fields are welcome. A clinical background is valuable but not required; the important thing is a serious interest in the problem and willingness to learn the domain.

Do I need machine learning experience?

It depends on the project level. For PhD projects, strong evidence of technical preparation is expected. For Honours and coursework projects, relevant programming, mathematics or data-science foundations can be enough.

Are scholarships available?

Scholarships and admission criteria are administered by UWA. Strong prospective HDR candidates should review UWA’s higher degree by research information and discuss possible research fit with a co-director before applying.

Can my clinical group propose a project?

Yes. Please describe the decision or measurement problem, the data available, existing ethics or governance arrangements, and what a useful outcome would look like. We can then assess technical fit together.

Have a project in mind?

We would be glad to hear from prospective students, clinical collaborators and research partners with a well-defined question.
