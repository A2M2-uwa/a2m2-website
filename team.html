An expanding group

Building the next generation of clinical AI researchers

A²M² brings together research students, clinical partners and technical collaborators around well-defined medical problems. We value careful methods, clear communication and the willingness to work across disciplinary boundaries.

The group is growing. If you are interested in joining as a student or exploring a collaboration, we would be pleased to hear from you.
